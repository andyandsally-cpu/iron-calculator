Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026
Short version: This tool runs entirely in your browser. Your health data — ferritin levels, period details, supplement doses — is never sent to any server. It stays on your device only.

What data the tool collects

The Iron Balance Tool is a static web application that runs entirely in your browser. It does not have a server, a database, or a user account system.

The following information you enter stays on your device only, stored in your browser's local storage:

  • Ferritin levels and blood test dates you enter
  • Period and cycle information
  • Supplement doses and formulation
  • Condition toggles and preferences
  • Session save data (if you use the save feature)

This data is never transmitted anywhere. Clearing your browser data or local storage will delete it permanently.

Payment data

If you purchase the full version, payment is processed by Stripe, a third-party payment processor. We do not see or store your card details at any point — these are handled entirely by Stripe's secure payment system.

After a successful payment, a simple unlock flag ("ironToolUnlocked: true") is stored in your browser's local storage. No personal or payment information is stored in this flag.

Stripe's privacy policy is available at stripe.com/privacy.

Analytics and tracking

This tool does not currently use any analytics, tracking cookies, or third-party scripts other than those required to process payments via Stripe.

The GP discussion summary

The GP discussion summary feature generates a draft letter from the information you have entered into the tool. This draft exists only in your browser — it is not sent anywhere automatically. You copy it yourself and send it to your GP directly. You are responsible for reviewing and editing the content before sending.

Data retention

All data is stored in your browser's local storage. It is retained until you clear your browser data or local storage. We have no access to this data and cannot delete it on your behalf — only you can do this through your browser settings.

Children

This tool is not intended for use by children under 16 without parental supervision. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or store any of your personal data on our servers, there is nothing for us to provide, correct, or delete on your behalf. All data is under your own control in your browser.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can contact us at ironbalancetool@gmail.com.
