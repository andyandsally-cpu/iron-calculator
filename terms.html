1. Nature of this tool

The Iron Balance Tool is an educational and personal planning tool. It is designed to help you:

  • Understand how iron balance and ferritin work
  • Estimate your approximate iron losses and absorption based on information you provide
  • Prepare questions and information for a conversation with your GP or clinician

It is not a medical device, a diagnostic tool, or a prescribing system. It does not provide clinical recommendations.

2. Not medical advice

All outputs from this tool — including calculations, projections, the GP discussion summary, and any suggested approaches — are estimates based on population-average data and information you have entered. They are provided for personal planning and GP discussion purposes only.

Nothing in this tool constitutes medical advice. You must not rely on outputs from this tool to make decisions about your health without first discussing them with a qualified healthcare professional.

3. The GP discussion summary

The GP discussion summary feature generates a draft document from the information you have entered. You are solely responsible for:

  • Reading and reviewing the draft in full before sending it to anyone
  • Editing or correcting any information that is inaccurate or does not reflect your situation
  • Deciding whether to send it and to whom

This draft is a starting point for a conversation — not a clinical document or formal referral. Your GP may disagree with, modify, or disregard any information it contains, and that is appropriate.

4. Accuracy of calculations

All calculations are based on population-average absorption rates, menstrual loss estimates, and published clinical reference ranges. Individual results vary significantly. The tool cannot account for all factors affecting iron metabolism, including:

  • Individual variation in absorption
  • Concurrent medications
  • Inflammation or infection affecting ferritin levels
  • Conditions not captured by the available toggles
  • Haemoglobin, transferrin saturation, or other blood markers
